## Project Summary and Goals

Use salvaged solar panels tied directly to an electric resistance water heating element in a tank of water. Since the space is so small, simply warming up the metal tank of water will heat the space. Heating can be dampened by putting removable insulation (a "jacket") on the tank during the day, and removing the jacket at night.

A key with using salvaged systems is to match PV array configuration to heating element resistance to achieve maximum heating energy delivered to the heating element. 

## Specs

- PV: (6) 120w 12v panels, salvaged, 20yo
- Tank: 14g stainless steel "fusti" tank, left over from another project
- Heating Element: 900w, 24v, 37.5a
- The system operates at around 450w (I need to return to the system to get up to date measured data).
